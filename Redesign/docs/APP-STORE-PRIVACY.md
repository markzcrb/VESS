# App Store privacy: labels, policy text, review notes

## Privacy nutrition label (App Store Connect)
| Data type | Collected | Linked to the user | Used for tracking | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Search History | Yes: food search words and scanned barcodes are sent to Open Food Facts to find products | No | No | App Functionality |

Everything else VESS stores (profile, food log, drinks, workouts, pantry, plans, supplements, cycle days, habits) stays on the iPhone and is never sent anywhere, so it isn't "collected" in Apple's sense. Apple Health data is read on the device and not kept. No account, analytics, ads or third-party SDKs.

## Privacy policy text (to add to the published policy)
> **Open Food Facts.** When you search for a food or scan a barcode, VESS sends the search words or the barcode to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to find the product. Nothing else is sent: no account, name, location or health data. Products found are kept on your iPhone so they work offline. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to these requests.
>
> **Cycle tracking.** Off unless you turn it on. Cycle days stay on your iPhone: they're never sent anywhere and never shared with Apple Health. You can export them with Export my data and remove them with Delete cycle data or Delete all data.

## Review notes (App Review Information › Notes)
> Cycle tracking is opt-in (You › Preferences). Cycle data is stored only on the device, is not sent to any server and is not shared with HealthKit. The estimate is labelled as an estimate, not medical advice and not contraception; there are no fertility, ovulation or pregnancy features. HealthKit is read-only (steps, workouts, active energy, weight).
