# App Store privacy: labels, policy text, review notes

The AI processor is named in the app from one constant, `AIProcessor.displayName` (now "Google Gemini"). The server chooses the provider (`AI_PROVIDER`, see `supabase/README.md`). **Processor today: Google (Gemini API).** If we switch to Claude, this changes to **Anthropic (Claude API)**, in this file, in the published policy and in the app constant, and the consent version is bumped so everyone is asked again.

Everything VESS stores (profile, food log, drinks, workouts, pantry, plans, supplements, cycle days, habits, the Coach conversation, the user's own and saved AI recipes with their photos) stays on the iPhone. The server keeps only the account (user ID and email) and a per-day count of AI uses (`ai_usage`). Apple Health data (read only: steps, workouts, active energy, weight, body fat, waist circumference, sleep analysis, heart rate, resting heart rate, walking heart rate average, heart rate variability, State of Mind and mindful minutes) is read on the device and not kept; nothing from it leaves the iPhone except steps and recorded workouts (type, minutes, kcal) in Coach's summary, when the user asks. Sleep, Heart and Stress are worked out on the device. The Pregnant switch (You › Body) is stored only on the iPhone. No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.

**Third-party processor:** Google (Gemini API), for Coach answers, photo estimates and AI Chef recipes, through VESS's server (Supabase, EU, Frankfurt). Google processes the requests under its API terms. If the provider changes to Anthropic (Claude API), update this line.

## Privacy policy text (to add to the published policy)
> **Account.** You only need an account for Coach, photo estimates and AI Chef. You sign in with a one-time code by email (or Sign in with Apple). We keep your email address (or Apple's private relay address), your account ID and a count of how many AI requests you made each day, so we can apply the free daily limits. Your food log and other data are not stored on our server. You can delete your account at any time in You › Account; this removes your email, account ID and usage counts from our server. Data on your iPhone stays until you also choose Delete all data.
>
> **Coach.** Coach is optional. Before your first question, VESS asks for your consent. When you ask, VESS sends your question, the conversation so far, and a summary of your recent logs (your targets, today's totals, 7-day averages, today's meals, habits, workouts, supplements and steps; your cycle day and next estimated window only if you turn on Share cycle with Coach; and that you're pregnant, only if you've switched on Pregnant in You › Body, so Coach doesn't suggest losing weight) through VESS's server to Google's Gemini API for an answer. VESS doesn't store your questions, the summary or the answers on its server; the conversation is kept only on your iPhone, and you can clear it in Coach. Google processes the request under its API terms. You can see exactly what is sent in Coach › What Coach sees, and turn Coach off in You › Privacy and data.
>
> **AI Chef.** AI Chef is optional. Before the first recipe, VESS asks for your consent. When you tap Create recipe, VESS sends your pantry list (item names and which ones to use soon), the choices you made (must-use items, meal, goal, servings, only what I have), your diet, the ingredients you don't eat, your allergies and today's remaining protein through VESS's server to Google's Gemini API to write one recipe. Nothing else from your log is sent. VESS doesn't store the request or the recipe on its server. Nutrition is calculated on your iPhone from USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts, not taken from the AI, and VESS checks the recipe against your allergies and the ingredients you don't eat before showing it. The recipe is saved only if you save it, and then only on your iPhone. You can turn AI Chef off in You › Privacy and data.
>
> **Photo estimates.** Photo estimates are optional. Before the first one, VESS asks for your consent. When you take or pick a photo, VESS makes it smaller, removes its location and camera details, and sends it once through VESS's server to Google's Gemini API to estimate the food in it. VESS doesn't store the photo, on the server or on your iPhone. Nothing is added to your log until you review and confirm the estimate. Google processes the request under its API terms. You can turn photo estimates off in You › Privacy and data.
>
> **Open Food Facts.** When you search for a food or scan a barcode, VESS sends the search words or the barcode to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) to find the product. Nothing else is sent: no account, name, location or health data. Products found are kept on your iPhone so they work offline. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies to these requests.
>
> **Apple Health.** VESS only reads from Apple Health, and only the types you allow: steps, workouts, active energy, weight, body fat, waist circumference, sleep, heart rate (resting, walking and heart rate variability), State of Mind and mindful minutes. It never writes to Health. These readings are shown and worked out on your iPhone (for example the sleep, heart and stress screens, which are estimates and not medical measurements) and VESS doesn't keep a copy. None of it is sent anywhere, except your steps and recorded workouts in Coach's summary when you ask Coach a question.
>
> **Pregnant.** You can switch on Pregnant in You › Body. It's stored only on your iPhone. While it's on, VESS hides BMI and anything about losing weight, and Coach's summary includes that you're pregnant so Coach doesn't suggest losing weight.
>
> **Cycle tracking.** Off unless you turn it on. Cycle days stay on your iPhone and are never shared with Apple Health. They're sent nowhere, except that Coach receives your current cycle day and the next estimated window with a question if you turn on Share cycle with Coach (off by default). You can export them with Export my data and remove them with Delete cycle data or Delete all data.

## Consent (guideline 5.1.2(i))
- Before the first Coach question, the first photo estimate and the first AI Chef recipe, a one-time sheet says what is sent (Coach: the question, the conversation and a summary of recent logs; photo: the photo; AI Chef: the pantry list, the recipe choices, diet, avoid list, allergies and today's protein gap), to whom ("Google Gemini, through VESS's server"), and that "VESS doesn't store it. Google processes it under its API terms." Buttons: Allow / Not now. Not now sends nothing.
- Consent is stored per feature and per processor (consent version). Switching provider bumps the version, so the sheet is shown again.
- Revocable at any time in You › Privacy and data › AI features.

## Account deletion (guideline 5.1.1(v))
- You › Account › Delete account calls the `delete-account` function, which deletes the user in Supabase Auth; the `ai_usage` rows go with it (on delete cascade). The app then signs out.
- The dialog offers "Delete account" (the log stays on the iPhone) and "Delete account and all data" (also runs Delete all data).

## Review notes (App Review Information › Notes)
> Coach, photo estimates and AI Chef are optional and need a free account. To review them, use Sign in with Apple (You › Account). Sign-in with a one-time code by email also exists, as a fallback. Before the first use of each, the app asks for consent and names the processor (Google Gemini, through our server). Requests are forwarded once and not stored by us; the server keeps only the account and a daily usage counter (free beta limits: Coach 20, photos 10, AI Chef recipes 10 per day, counted per UTC day). Nothing from a photo estimate is logged and no AI Chef recipe is saved without the user's confirmation; AI Chef recipes are checked on the device against the user's allergies, and their nutrition is calculated on the device. Accounts can be deleted in the app (You › Account › Delete account).
>
> Cycle tracking is opt-in (You › Preferences). Cycle data is stored only on the device and is not shared with HealthKit; it is sent to Coach only if the user turns on "Share cycle with Coach" (off by default). The estimate is labelled as an estimate, not medical advice and not contraception; there are no fertility or ovulation features. A Pregnant switch (You › Body, stored on the device) only hides BMI and weight-loss wording and tells Coach not to suggest weight loss; there is no pregnancy tracking. HealthKit is read-only (steps, workouts, active energy, weight, body fat, waist circumference, sleep analysis, heart rate, resting and walking heart rate, heart rate variability, State of Mind, mindful minutes). Sleep, heart and stress screens are on-device estimates, labelled as not medical measurements; no HealthKit data is sent off the device except steps and recorded workouts in the Coach summary.
