# UMEVA · Discover recipes (v3, with nutrition)

122 original recipes for Discover. The full data the app ships (amounts, steps, sources) is `Redesign/Data/recipes.json`; this file is the readable overview.

**Nutrition** is per serving, calculated by code from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy) per ingredient weight. A few EU products missing from USDA (skyr, quark, oat drink, coconut drink, halloumi, harissa, mirin, rice paper, smoked mackerel/trout, hot-smoked salmon, buckwheat flakes, matcha, digestive biscuits, light coconut milk, frozen mixed berries) use typical EU label values and are marked in the JSON.

Diet tags: **VG** vegan · **V** vegetarian · **P** pescatarian · **O** meat · **GF** gluten-free · **DF** dairy-free · **HP** high-protein = protein ≥ 20 g per serving, or ≥ 15 g and ≥ 30 % of energy (calculated).
Allergens use the EU 14: gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, peanuts, soy, milk, nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites, lupin, molluscs. They are the union of the ingredients' allergens.
